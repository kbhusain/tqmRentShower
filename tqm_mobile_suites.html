The trailer requires two items for standard operation:

1. Electricity: One 30-amp 110V/120V dedicated circuit (or two separate 20-amp circuits) to run the air conditioning, hot water heating elements, and vanity lighting.
2. Water Supply: A standard outdoor water hose connection (3/4" garden spigot) providing at least 40–50 PSI.

If your location is primitive or lacks power and water, we offer complete off-grid power generator packages and auxiliary fresh water tanks.
The unit features massive commercial holding tanks designed to handle hundreds of showers and flushes. If your site has a sewer cleanout or dump station, we can hook up directly with standard camlock drain hoses. For multi-day or long-term industrial rentals, we schedule periodic vacuum pumping service so you never have to worry about overflow.
The trailer is approximately 20 feet long (24 feet including the hitch tongue) and 8.5 feet wide. When the fold-down aluminum entrance steps and handrails are extended, you will need approximately 12 feet of total width clearance. We require a level parking area (concrete, asphalt, hard-packed gravel, or firm dry grass).
